    Rooted in Entebbe, on the shores of Lake Victoria

    Born of local passion, guided by conservation & cultural heritage

    Openbill Safaris Uganda SMC-LTD takes its name and guiding ethos from the African Openbill Stork (Anastomus lamelligerus)—an iconic wading bird native to Uganda's pristine wetlands whose specialized bill was sculpted by nature to unlock freshwater mussels with patient precision. In that same spirit, we bring the native tracking heritage, field patience, and specialized expertise required to unlock the most authentic, untamed wildlife encounters across Uganda, Rwanda, Tanzania, and Kenya.

    Founded and run by native Ugandan safari naturalists, every expedition we curate is 100% private and tailor-made around your pace. We deploy our own customized 4x4 Land Cruiser fleet with pop-up photography roofs, guarantee direct Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) gorilla and chimpanzee permit acquisition, and proudly direct a percentage of every safari booking to our registered charitable arm, the OpenVision Foundation Uganda, funding schooling, clean water, and ranger families in communities bordering our national parks.

    Native-Born Naturalists UWA & USAGA certified driver-guides with lifelong tracking mastery.
    Custom 4x4 Fleet Long-wheelbase Land Cruisers with 360° pop-up roofs & onboard power.
    Guaranteed Primate Permits Direct statutory allocation with UWA and RDB authorities.
    OpenVision Charity Travel for a cause—funding village schooling, clean water & clinic care.
