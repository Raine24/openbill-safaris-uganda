About

Openbill Safaris Uganda

Openbill Safaris is your gateway to unforgettable adventures in Uganda and beyond. We specialize in immersive wildlife safaris, gorilla trekking, cultural tours, and tailor-made experiences that bring you closer to Africa’s raw beauty.

With expert guides, seamless planning, and a passion for sustainable tourism, we create safaris that leave lasting memories. Whether you’re chasing the Big Five, exploring vibrant cultures, or cruising along scenic landscapes, Openbill Safaris ensures every journey is nothing short of extraordinary. Discover, explore, and experience Africa with us!

We are a fully registered and Licensed Tour Operator by Uganda Tourism Board. License Number(UTB/RTT/TO/2026/102199)
