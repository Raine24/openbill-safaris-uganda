OVERVIEW:
OpenVision Foundation Uganda is a registered non-governmental organization established as the charitable arm of Openbill Safaris Uganda. The Foundation is dedicated to empowering vulnerable rural populations, supporting children's education and health programs, and promoting environmental conservation along the borders of Uganda's national parks.

KEY INITIATIVES:
1. "TRAVEL FOR A CAUSE" REVENUE SHARING:
   A guaranteed percentage of the operating profits from every safari booked with Openbill Safaris Uganda is directly donated to the OpenVision Foundation Uganda. Travelers contribute to grassroots change simply by booking their African safari.

2. EDUCATION SPONSORSHIP:
   Funding school tuition, uniforms, textbooks, and scholastic materials for over 120 orphans and vulnerable children living in rural communities adjacent to Bwindi Impenetrable, Kibale Forest, and Queen Elizabeth National Parks.

3. RURAL HEALTHCARE & MATERNAL CLINICS:
   Providing essential medical supplies, malaria mosquito nets, and clean birthing kits to remote health centers in western and northern Uganda.

4. CLEAN WATER & SANITATION:
   Constructing deep boreholes and solar-powered rainwater harvesting systems to provide clean, disease-free drinking water to communities bordering wildlife reserves.

5. COMMUNITY CONSERVATION PARTNERSHIPS:
   Training local youth as certified bird guides, porters, and conservation ambassadors—providing sustainable livelihood alternatives to illegal bushmeat poaching and deforestation.
