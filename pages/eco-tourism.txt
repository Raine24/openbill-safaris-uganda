As Uganda's premier eco-tour company, Openbill Safaris operates under a strict conservation-first ethos designed to protect the natural ecosystems of East Africa for generations to come.

OUR ECO-TOURISM PRINCIPLES:
1. STRICT PRIMATE VIEWING ETHICS:
   We adhere strictly to the Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) guidelines:
   - Maintaining a minimum distance of 7 to 10 meters from mountain gorillas and chimpanzees.
   - Mandatory medical face masks during primate viewing to prevent transmission of human airborne pathogens.
   - Strictly capping viewing time to sixty (60) minutes with habituated gorilla families.
   - Prohibiting flash photography under all circumstances.

2. LEAVE NO TRACE WILDERNESS TRAVEL:
   All single-use plastics are strictly prohibited inside our safari vehicles. We provide refillable stainless-steel water containers and coordinate with eco-certified safari lodges that generate solar energy, recycle greywater, and compost organic waste.

3. LOCAL ECONOMIC EMPOWERMENT:
   We believe wildlife conservation only succeeds when communities thrive. 100% of our driver-guides, camp staff, trackers, and porters are local Ugandans paid fair, competitive living wages. We actively promote community cultural tours in Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary, Batwa cultural trails, and Karamojong villages where tour fees go directly to village elders and women's cooperatives.

4. HABITAT CONSERVATION & ANTI-POACHING:
   Through our partnership with UWA and the OpenVision Foundation Uganda, a portion of each tour booking funds ranger patrol equipment, tree-planting buffer zone reforestations, and snare-removal patrols along park boundaries.
