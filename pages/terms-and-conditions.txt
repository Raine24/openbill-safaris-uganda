5. CANCELLATION & REFUND POLICY
All cancellation notices must be submitted in writing via email to openbillsafarisuganda@gmail.com. The effective date of cancellation is the date of receipt by the Company.

Cancellation Fee Schedule:
• More than 60 days before departure: Full refund of safari funds paid, EXCEPT non-refundable statutory gorilla and chimpanzee permits.
• 45 to 59 days before departure: 30% cancellation fee of total safari package cost.
• 30 to 44 days before departure: 50% cancellation fee of total safari package cost.
• 15 to 29 days before departure: 75% cancellation fee of total safari package cost.
• Less than 15 days before departure or No-Show: 100% cancellation fee (no refund issued).

Special Note on Force Majeure:
In the event that travel cannot proceed due to verifiable force majeure (e.g. government border closures, pandemic restrictions, political unrest, or natural catastrophes), the Company will reschedule the safari within a twelve (12) month window at no administrative penalty, subject to third-party lodge availability and statutory permit rollover rules.

• Primate Health Protocol: Mountain gorillas and chimpanzees share over 98% human DNA and are highly susceptible to human respiratory illnesses. Clients showing symptoms of flu, cough, or infectious illness may be barred by UWA park authorities from trekking to protect wild populations.
