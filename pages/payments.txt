--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For seamless and immediate confirmation, we accept major international payment cards through our secure licensed payment gateway:

3. CASH ON ARRIVAL GUIDELINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Balance payments may be settled in cash upon arrival in Entebbe/Kampala under the following strict conditions:
• Prior Authorization: Must be authorized in writing in your booking contract by the Managing Director / CEO.
• Currency: Clean, crisp US Dollars (USD) or Euros (EUR).
• Critical Banknote Policy: By regulation of the Bank of Uganda and commercial foreign exchange bureaus, US Dollar banknotes must be series 2009 or newer, free from tears, stamps, ink marks, or heavy creasing. Older dollar notes (prior to 2009) or damaged notes are strictly rejected across East Africa.
